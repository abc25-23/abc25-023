About Tsholo's Kitchen

Our Story

Tsholo’s Kitchen was founded on 2024 on the belief that everyone deserves a home-cooked meal that nourishes the soul. What started as a small family gathering passion project in Gaborone has grown into a space where the community comes together to share stories and incredible flavors."


Our Mission

At Tsholo’s Kitchen, we focus on fresh, locally sourced ingredients. Our mission is to provide authentic, high-quality meals that celebrate our heritage while offering a warm, welcoming environment for all our guests.


We believe that the secret to great food is patience and love. Every dish that leaves our kitchen is crafted with attention to detail, ensuring that each bite reminds you of home.
